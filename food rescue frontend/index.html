About FoodRescue

FoodRescue is a community-based food waste management platform designed to reduce the amount of edible food that is thrown away.

The platform allows individuals, restaurants, hotels and organizations to donate surplus food.

NGOs and volunteers can view available donations, claim food and coordinate collection.

Our Mission

Reduce food waste while helping communities access surplus food safely and efficiently.

Donation Guidelines

  • Food should be safe for consumption.
  • Mention accurate expiry information.
  • Keep food properly packed.
  • Provide an accurate pickup location.
  • Avoid donating spoiled food.
